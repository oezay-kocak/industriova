# Resource Progression Plan — 2026-09-07

Status: **Design and implementation plan only.** This document and its machine-readable companion do not change runtime behavior, saves, tests, or UI.

## Repository basis

The plan is derived from the active definitions in `src/production_zero/data/game_definitions.json` and `src/production_zero/data/resource_definitions.json`. Its runtime assumptions come from `src/production_zero/core/progression.py` (current initial and Lifetime unlock behavior), `src/production_zero/core/company.py` (unbounded levels, XP curve and title milestones), `src/production_zero/core/production_guide.py`, the Factory selection views, and the save paths documented in `docs/resource_progression_unlock_audit_2026-09-07.md`.

## Decision

Production Zero should use the audited hybrid model:

1. **Company Level discovers inputless Extractor families.** The current nine starting resources remain available at Level 1.
2. **Lifetime Production masters direct inputs.** Processor, Fabricator and Assembler options unlock one step at a time.
3. **Every direct input is mandatory.** A downstream option requires each direct input option to be unlocked and the stated lifetime amount to be produced for each one.
4. **Cross-family recipes wait for every family.** Their effective Company Level is the highest level among all extractor-root ancestors.
5. **Research remains separate.** None of the 353 baseline resources needs Research or a second currency.

The existing Company level is unbounded. Up to Level 99, `xp_required_for_next_level(level)` is `500 + 125 × level + 18 × level²`; titles exist at Levels 1, 10, 25, 50, 75 and 100. This plan uses Levels **1–25** for baseline raw-material discovery and deliberately lands Uranium on the existing **Industrialist** milestone at Level 25. Company progression remains useful after Level 25 for titles and future systems, while all baseline roots are then discoverable.

## Unlock rules

### Root discovery

An inputless Extractor option unlocks when `company.level >= family.company_level`. It has no Lifetime Production prerequisite. Level unlocks are monotone: once discovered, a root remains unlocked.

### Direct-input mastery

Each downstream option uses one fixed integer threshold per direct input:

| Unlocking stage | Requirement for **each** direct input | Base-rate interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Processor | 120 lifetime units | About one minute from a base Extractor producing 12 every 6 seconds. |
| Fabricator | 60 lifetime units | Usually one to six minutes depending on the Processor output amount; enough to operate the branch without a grind wall. |
| Assembler | 30 lifetime units | Usually three minutes at the common one-unit-per-cycle rate; complex multi-input recipes progress in parallel. |

The amount refers to **lifetime output produced**, never current inventory, sales, purchases, or freight received. A recipe with three inputs requires the threshold independently for all three. There are no multipliers by rarity, market value, input quantity, or family; keeping the rule fixed makes it predictable in the Guide.

An option unlocks only when all of these are true:

- Company Level is at least the highest discovery level of every extractor-root ancestor;
- every direct input's own production option is unlocked;
- lifetime production for every direct input meets the stage threshold.

This ordering matters for saves containing purchased or legacy stock: inventory alone cannot skip a production chain.

### Cross-family recipes

`extractor_families` is the complete transitive set of root families used by a resource. `family` in the JSON is a primary display/ownership family chosen deterministically as the latest-gated ancestor family; it is never a substitute for the full set. A cross-family recipe unlocks only after **all** families in `extractor_families` are discovered and all direct inputs meet the normal gate. No extra currency or hidden condition exists.

Example: if a recipe needs one unlocked Nickel-derived input and one unlocked High-Purity Silicon input, the player must have discovered both corresponding root families, unlocked both direct inputs, and produced the required lifetime amount of both.

## Production Guide and Factory Resource Selection

The Production Guide should keep all definitions discoverable as a structured catalog, but collapse distant content by default. Each locked row should show the first unmet reasons in this order:

1. `REQUIRES COMPANY LEVEL 16` for an undiscovered root family or a recipe whose latest root family is not yet available.
2. `REQUIRES BATTERY & ELECTRONICS FEEDSTOCKS FAMILY` when the level is met but a required root discovery has not been normalized yet.
3. `REQUIRES UNLOCKED INPUT: HIGH-PURITY SILICON` when a direct prerequisite option is still locked.
4. `PRODUCE 42 MORE COPPER ORE (78 / 120)` for each unlocked direct input below its Lifetime target.

For multiple unmet inputs, show at most two inline and add `+N MORE REQUIREMENTS`; the detail panel lists all of them. The next actionable item in each discovered family receives `NEXT`. Distant items remain under `LATER`, grouped by family and stage, so 353 rows do not confront the player at once.

Factory Resource Selection remains simpler: it lists only unlocked options compatible with that machine stage. A short empty-state link opens the Guide at the nearest actionable prerequisite. It must not expose locked endgame recipes or infer unlocks from owned inventory.

## Save migration design

The later implementation should use a monotone, versioned migration:

1. Add a progression schema version; load current `lifetime_produced`, `unlocked_production_ids`, `pending_unlock_ids`, Company Level and active machine selections without mutation first.
2. Build a **grandfather set** as the union of saved unlocked IDs, every currently selected production ID, and every output demonstrably produced in the save. Never remove an ID from this set even if the new plan would gate it later.
3. Add every Extractor root whose family level is at or below the saved Company Level.
4. Re-evaluate downstream gates to a fixed point using saved Lifetime Production plus the grandfather set. Inventory is not added to lifetime for modern saves. The existing conservative inventory fallback remains only for legacy saves that lack production-progress data.
5. Persist the normalized union atomically. Migration-generated unlocks do not enter `pending_unlock_ids`; this prevents an event-feed storm on first load.
6. Unknown historical IDs remain preserved in raw save data but are ignored by current selection UI. A backup/transaction rollback protects against malformed data.

Grandfathering is intentionally one-way. A Level-8 save already running an advanced recipe keeps it and its required selected option; it does not automatically receive that recipe's entire late family. Future unlocks continue under the new rules.

## Conceptual progression simulation

### Early game — Levels 1–5

The player begins with the current nine roots across metals/carbon, construction minerals, forestry, wheat and petroleum. Within roughly one base minute of operating a root, its Processor target can become actionable. Levels 2–5 add three or four familiar roots at a time: field crops, process minerals, food/fiber crops, then gas/elastomers. Processor choices become visible early, Fabricator choices follow after the first processed batches, and only actively operated branches reach Assembler gates. This keeps the selectable list narrow even though the Guide can preview later families.

### Mid game — Levels 7–16

Chemical minerals and common/advanced alloy metals arrive after short mastery windows, followed by carbon/specialty minerals and battery/electronics feedstocks. Cross-family recipes now become the main planning layer: their messages point to a missing family, direct input or remaining lifetime quantity. The fixed 120/60/30 rule lets parallel factories advance several branches without opaque scaling.

### Late game — Levels 19–25 and beyond

Gold/silver, catalytic precious metals, aerospace/refractory metals and finally uranium arrive in small, legible groups. Uranium at the Industrialist milestone prevents nuclear production from appearing in the early economy. At Level 25 every raw family is discoverable; continued play resolves remaining direct-input mastery gates until all 353 options are available. Higher unlimited Company Levels remain available for titles and future meta systems without blocking baseline resources.

## Validation and risk review

The accompanying JSON was generated from the active `game_definitions.json` and `resource_definitions.json`, not from a hand-maintained name list. Validation establishes:

- **353/353** resource definitions have exactly one production option and one plan entry.
- Stage counts remain **49 Extractor, 68 Processor, 86 Fabricator and 150 Assembler**.
- Every Extractor root belongs to exactly one of **18** families.
- Every non-root prerequisite resolves to a production option; no cycle exists.
- A topological simulation exposes all Level-25 roots, then repeatedly satisfies direct-input gates; it reaches **353/353** resources.
- There are **243** direct cross-family gates; **253** resources have more than one root family in their transitive origin. Each uses the same all-input rule, so none can form a family deadlock.
- There are **304** lifetime-gated resources containing **708** explicit per-input conditions.
- The largest post-start root drop is **5** resources. Large downstream cohorts at a family level are eligibility cohorts only; they remain individually paced by direct production.

No progression hole or Cross-Family deadlock remains in the proposed graph. The main balance risk is contract-driven Company XP pacing between Levels 16 and 25. Before implementation, a playtime simulation should measure real contract completion time and may move whole families by one or two levels; it must not alter dependency rules or put Uranium before the late band.
