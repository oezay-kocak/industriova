# Recipe cards: retirement and future direction

Recipe cards are no longer collectible cards. Only Energy, Freight and Machine cards participate in inventory, crafting, supply, packs and developer card creation. The former recipe-specific Daily Supply lane and pack category are removed.

Existing machine production options and their stable production IDs remain intact. They describe production rather than owned cards; the existing production progression is unchanged.

Legacy saves discard recipe-card inventory entries, last-opened recipe rewards and the old Daily Recipe Supply fields when loaded. Slots referencing discarded cards are cleared. Other card types, unopened packs, current Freight/Machine daily purchases, resources and production progression are preserved. There is no automatic cash or pack compensation.

Future design: recipes return as permanent knowledge / production unlocks in a Technology or Talent Tree. Basic early-game recipes become available early; complex recipes follow progression, talent points or technology advancement. They will not return as collectible cards. This change implements no new research UI, tree or unlock mechanism.
